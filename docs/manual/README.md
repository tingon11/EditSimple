The same manual opens inside the program, in the language of the interface:
**Manual** in the bar on the left (this folder is bundled in the executable).

The screenshots in `images/<language>/` (WebP) show the interface in that
language, taken by `scripts/manual_screenshots.py`, which rebuilds the
example projects step by step. The example video is
“[Scena Urbana Affollata con Persone che Camminano](https://www.pexels.com/it-it/video/scena-urbana-affollata-con-persone-che-camminano-29799947/)”
by [dp singh Bhullar](https://www.pexels.com/@dpsinghbhullar/), used under the
[Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The people in it are
ordinary passers-by who are doing nothing wrong: the effects placed on them
only show how the program works. The two motorbike photographs of the Images
page are by [Andrea Dalle Piatte](https://www.pexels.com/it-it/@andreadallepiatte/),
also published on Pexels under the same license. The background sound in the
audio example is a synthetic tone generated with ffmpeg. The picture of the
Word document shows a figure inserted by the program's **Word** button.

The translations other than Italian were made with the help of a language
model and have not been reviewed by native speakers yet: corrections are
welcome.
